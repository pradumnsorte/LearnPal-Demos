In-video AI support, three paradigms
LearnPal

Three concepts for intermittent, continuous, and proactive human–AI interaction in video-based learning.

01 · Intermittent

Learner-initiated.

The AI is silent until you call on it — through chat, or by snapping a region of the video to ask about.

Open prototype
02 · Continuous

Continuously available.

Glossary, highlights, and questions accumulate ambiently in side panels as the video plays. You sample what you want.

Open prototype
03 · Proactive

System-initiated.

Keyword popups, on-frame overlays, and pop quizzes interrupt at chosen learning moments. You can override; the system pushes.
